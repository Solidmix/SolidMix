### Frequently Asked Questions — RMC Services in Raipur

## Everything you need to know before ordering ready mix concrete.

#### What are RMC services and what do they include?

RMC (Ready Mix Concrete) services cover the full process of producing, delivering, and supporting the placement of factory-batched concrete. At SolidMix, our RMC services in Raipur include custom mix design, precision batching at our plant, transit mixer delivery, on-site slump testing, and post-pour quality documentation.

#### What concrete grades does SolidMix supply in Raipur?

We supply standard grades from M15 to M40, as well as specialty mixes including waterproof concrete, fiber-reinforced concrete (PP and steel fiber), eco-friendly concrete, self-compacting concrete, and heat-resistant concrete. View all our ready mix concrete products [link: /products/] for full details.

#### What is the minimum order quantity for RMC delivery in Raipur?

Our standard minimum order is 4 cubic metres per delivery. For smaller requirements, we also offer bagged concrete mix options. Contact our team [link: /contact-us/] to discuss your specific volume needs.

#### How far does SolidMix deliver ready mix concrete from its Raipur plant?

Our transit mixer fleet covers Raipur, Naya Raipur, and surrounding areas within approximately 30–40 km of our plant. For projects outside this radius, please contact us to discuss feasibility.

#### How do I calculate how much concrete I need for my project?

Use our free online concrete calculator [link: /calculator/] to get an instant volume estimate for slabs, columns, beams, and footings. You can also call our team for help with mix design and volume planning.

#### What quality checks does SolidMix perform on its concrete?

Every batch undergoes slump testing at the plant and cube casting for 7-day and 28-day compressive strength tests per IS 456 standards. Our quality head supervises all testing, and records are available to clients on request. Learn more about our safety and quality standards.
