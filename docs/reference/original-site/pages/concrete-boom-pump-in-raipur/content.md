A Boom Pump is a truck-mounted concrete pumping system equipped with a robotic arm (boom) that transports concrete directly to the required placement area. Compared to traditional methods, a Boom Pump significantly reduces project delays, minimizes material wastage, and enhances construction quality.

When construction sites have limited accessibility, a Boom Pump becomes the most practical solution for delivering concrete precisely where it is needed.

## What Is a Boom Pump and How Does It Work?

A Boom Pump consists of a hydraulic arm mounted on a truck. The arm can extend vertically and horizontally to transport ready-mix concrete from the mixer directly to construction points.

### Boom Pump Working Process

- Ready-mix concrete is loaded into the pump hopper.

- Hydraulic cylinders push the concrete through delivery pipes.

- The boom arm directs concrete to the desired location.

- Concrete is placed accurately with minimal manual handling.

- Construction progresses faster with consistent output.

This process improves efficiency and reduces the challenges associated with manual transportation methods.
