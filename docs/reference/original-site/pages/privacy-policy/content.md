## Solidmix RMC – Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Solidmix RMC uses cookies to improve your website experience.

### What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored on your device when you visit a website. These files help improve functionality and gather insights to better serve you.

### Why We Use Cookies

- Essential Cookies: Enable core website functions like page navigation and secure access.

- Performance Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site for better optimization.

- Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences to offer a personalized experience.

- Advertising Cookies: May be used to show relevant ads and measure campaign performance.

### Types of Cookies We Use

- Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser.

- Persistent Cookies: Stay on your device until removed, storing preferences for future visits.

### Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies via your browser settings. Note that restricting cookies may limit site features or performance.

### Policy Updates

We may revise this Cookie Policy periodically. Please review it regularly to stay informed.
