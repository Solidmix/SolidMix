## Why Raipur's Contractors Choose SolidMix RMC

### 1. Computer-Controlled Batching — Zero Human Error

Our RMC plant runs on a fully automated batching system. The ratio of cement, aggregates, water, and admixtures is controlled by computer — meaning every transit mixer delivers exactly the same quality concrete, every single time. No excess water, no under-cement batches.

### 2. On-Time Delivery — No Idle Time on Site

A late concrete delivery means your entire crew sits idle. Our fleet of transit mixers delivers to any corner of Raipur within 60–90 minutes. Available 24×7, including weekends and public holidays.

### 3. IS 456:2000 Certified Quality

All our concrete mixes comply with Bureau of Indian Standards IS 456:2000 guidelines. Every batch goes through slump testing, cube testing, and water-cement ratio verification. See our full quality assurance process

### 4. Free Mix Design Consultation

Not sure which grade is right for your project? Our experienced engineers assess your project requirements and recommend the ideal concrete mix — completely free of charge. Book your free consultation

### 5. Minimal Wastage — Direct Cost Savings

With ready mix concrete, you order exactly what you need. Manual site mixing typically wastes 15–20% of material. With RMC, wastage is near zero — which directly reduces your overall project cost.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is the SolidMix RMC concrete plant located in Raipur?

Our plant is located at Ph.no.77, Kh.no.365/5, Serikhedi, Raipur, Chhattisgarh 492101. Our office is at Lalganga Business Park, Pachpedi Naka, Raipur. Both locations are available on Google Maps.

### What is the minimum order quantity for ready mix concrete?

Our minimum order is 6 cubic meters (one transit mixer load). If you need a smaller quantity, contact our team  we’ll find a solution.

[LINK] text="contact our team" href=https://solidmixrmc.com/contact-us/

### How long does concrete delivery take?

For most Raipur city areas, delivery takes 60–90 minutes after order confirmation. Delivery time may vary for distant locations. Call us in advance for urgent requirements.

### What concrete grades does SolidMix RMC supply?

We supply M20, M25, M30, M35, and M40 grade ready mix concrete. Free consultation is available to help you choose the right grade for your project.

[LINK] text="Free consultation" href=https://solidmixrmc.com/appointments/

### How is concrete quality tested?

Every batch undergoes a slump test at the plant. Cube samples are cast at the site and tested for compressive strength at 7 days and 28 days as per IS 456:2000. See our safety and quality page

[LINK] text="See our safety and quality page" href=https://solidmixrmc.com/safety/

### Does SolidMix RMC deliver on Sundays and public holidays?

Yes. We operate 24×7, 365 days a year — including Sundays, Diwali, and all public holidays. Construction doesn’t stop, and neither do we.
