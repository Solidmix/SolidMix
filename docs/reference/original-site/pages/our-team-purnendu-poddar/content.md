# Purnendu Poddar

## Head - Business Development

Purnendu Poddar is a seasoned professional with over 19 years of experience in the Indian cement industry. He has worked with leading companies such as Ultratech, Nuvoco, Emami Cement, and Star Cement. As a founding member of Double Bull Cement, Purnendu has demonstrated strong entrepreneurial and leadership capabilities. Specializing in supply chain strategy, he has also held key leadership roles in sales across channel and institutional segments. Purnendu holds an engineering degree in Electronics and Communication and an MBA, combining technical expertise with strategic business acumen.
