## Frequently Asked Questions

### What grades of ready mix concrete does SolidMix supply in Raipur?

We supply M15, M20, M25, M30, M35, and M40 grade concrete, as well as custom mixes for specialized structural requirements.

### What is the delivery range of your RMC plant in Raipur?

Our fleet covers Raipur, Naya Raipur, and surrounding areas in Chhattisgarh within a 30–40 km radius.

### How do I place an order for ready mix concrete?

### Is your concrete tested for quality before delivery?

We supply M20, M25, M30, M35, and M40 grade ready mix concrete. Free consultation is available to help you choose the right grade for your project.

### Yes. Every batch undergoes slump tests and cube strength tests as per IS 456 standards.

Every batch undergoes a slump test at the plant. Cube samples are cast at the site and tested for compressive strength at 7 days and 28 days as per IS 456:2000. See our safety and quality page
