SolidMix RMC: Changes, Menu and Contact Form
Overall summary, 23 November 2026. It lists what has changed, then the full menu and the full Book Us form, then what is still open.
1. List of changes done
2. Menu and submenu in detail
Top bar, left to right. The first line inside each dropdown is the overview page for that menu.
Also on every page
Highlighted button on the right of the header: Book Us.
Phone: a menu button that opens a drawer, and a bottom bar with Call, WhatsApp and Book Us.
Footer only: Privacy Policy, Legal Information.
Not in the menu yet because the pages do not exist: Concrete FAQ, Concrete Grades Guide.
3. Book Us form in detail
Aim: a site manager on a phone can finish it in under a minute. * means required. Every dropdown has "Not sure".
Four short blocks
1. About you: name, mobile, email, organisation name, designation.
2. What you need: what is this about, I am a, grade, pump.
3. Your site: nearest plant, area, site location.
4. When and how much (optional): quantity, pour date and time, anything else.
What happens after sending
The page shows a Lead ID and a Call button.
The team gets an email with the subject: Lead ID | Plant | Topic | Pump.
The lead is saved (storage still to be decided).
Rest of the Book Us page
Top: heading, Call and WhatsApp buttons.
Then the form.
Then one card per plant: address, phone, map and the areas it serves.
A small FAQ at the bottom.
