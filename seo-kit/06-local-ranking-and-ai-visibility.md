No one can guarantee position 1. These are the factors that decide it, in order of impact for "ready mix concrete in Raipur".

- [ ] Name exactly **SolidMix RMC** (no keywords added; that risks suspension).
- [ ] Primary category: Ready-mix concrete supplier. Secondary: Concrete product supplier, Building materials supplier (only those that apply).
- [ ] Address, phone, website, hours identical to the website footer and schema.
- [ ] Service area set to Raipur (add areas only once confirmed).
- [ ] Description (750 chars): what SolidMix supplies, where, how to order. Confirmed facts only.
- [ ] Products added (12) and Services added, with photos.
- [ ] 20+ real photos: plant, trucks, batching, cube testing, delivered sites. Add 2-4 new per month.
- [ ] Booking/quote link points to /get-a-quote/.
- [ ] Posts weekly (project, offer, tip). Q&A seeded with the real FAQ.
- [ ] Reviews: see section 4.

## 2. Citations (same name, address, phone everywhere)

- [ ] JustDial, IndiaMART, Sulekha, TradeIndia, ExportersIndia
- [ ] Bing Places, Apple Business Connect
- [ ] Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, X profiles (website link + same NAP)
- [ ] Construction/building directories and CREDAI / builders' association pages in Chhattisgarh if eligible

Record each in a sheet: site, URL, date, NAP matches (Y/N).

## 3. Backlinks and mentions
- Ask builders, architects and contractors the client already supplies for a "supplied by SolidMix RMC" mention with a link on their project pages.
- Local news or press note for the new plant, awards, milestones (only if true).
- Supplier/partner links (cement, admixture and equipment suppliers).
- Do not buy links or use link farms.

## 4. Reviews
- Direct Google review link and a QR code (Google Business Profile > Get more reviews).
- Process: salesperson sends the link by WhatsApp 1-3 days after a successful pour. Ask every customer, do not select only happy ones, do not offer rewards, do not write reviews for customers.
- Reply to every review within 48 hours, including negative ones, calmly.
- Target: steady flow (e.g. 4-8 per month), not a burst.

## 5. Bing and AI answer engines

- [ ] robots.txt allows GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended (already in `02-robots.txt`).
