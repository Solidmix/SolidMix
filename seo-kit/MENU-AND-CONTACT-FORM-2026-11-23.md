SolidMix RMC: Menu and Contact Form
Working document, 23 November 2026. Part 1 is the menu as it is live today, with a blank column for your changes. Part 2 is the Book Us form.
Part 1: Menu and submenu (as live today)
Top bar, left to right. The first line inside each dropdown is the overview page for that menu. Project Gallery sits under Our Projects.
Also on every page
Highlighted button on the right of the header: Book Us (goes to the contact page).
Phone: a menu button that opens a drawer, and a bottom bar with Call, WhatsApp and Book Us.
Footer only: Privacy Policy, Legal Information.
Not in the menu yet because the pages do not exist: Concrete FAQ, Concrete Grades Guide.
Option B: a shorter menu (suggestion only)
If the current menu feels crowded, this has six items instead of seven. Contact Us becomes the Book Us button, and Careers moves under About Us. Tell me if you want this, or mark up Part 1 instead.
Part 2: Book Us form (easier to fill)
The goal is that a site manager on a phone can finish it in under a minute. Seven things are required (marked *): name, mobile number, what the enquiry is about, I am a, grade, pump and nearest plant. Email, organisation name and designation sit right after the name and mobile number and are optional. Everything else is a dropdown or a button, and every dropdown has "Not sure". The team can ask the rest on the call.
Fields, in the order people see them
* means required.
Group them in four short blocks
1. About you: name, mobile, email, organisation name, designation.
2. What you need: what is this about, I am a, grade, pump.
3. Your site: nearest plant, area, site location.
4. When and how much (optional): quantity, pour date and time, anything else.
What makes it easy
Big tap targets and one column on a phone. The number keypad opens for the mobile number.
Grade and area lists show only what applies: no grade list for Others, and the area list changes with the plant.
After choosing a plant and area, one plain line says "This area is on our served list for this plant. Our team will confirm availability for your site." No delivery times, charges or distances.
If someone refuses "Use my location", the form still works.
The button says Book Us. After sending, the page shows a Lead ID (for example SM-261123-A4F9) and a Call button.
