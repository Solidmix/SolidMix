# Project Brief

## Project
Solid Mix website (new project). SolidMix RMC is a ready-mix concrete (RMC) company in Raipur, Chhattisgarh, with its plant at Serikhedi, near Rawabhata.

## Current site
https://solidmixrmc.com/ (WordPress, ~72 sitemap URLs): home, about, team, products list + 12 product pages, services, our work, calculator, safety, blogs, gallery, contact, appointments, careers, legal, privacy policy, blog posts, brochure PDF.

## Goal
Build an improved REPLICA of the site, page by page, with the same look and all the same information:
- Fix broken (404) pages and duplicate URLs with redirects.
- Fully mobile responsive with a better mobile experience.
- SEO friendly and AEO (AI answer engine) friendly.
- Add a "your area" dropdown to the Contact Us form.

## Important
The replica does NOT replace the original site. It is deployed on its own Vercel URL for review. The original WordPress site is never modified. Moving the replica to the solidmixrmc.com domain will be decided later, only after Ma'am's approval.
